# Cordis: Revertible Effects and Reactive Coeffects for Dynamic Composability

## Reference

**Authors**: Peking University & DeepSeek-AI

**Year**: 2026

**Abstract**: This paper provides the formal foundations for dynamic composability, introducing the concepts of revertible effects and reactive coeffects. It establishes the theoretical framework that VeriLinkOS builds upon to create a production control plane for autonomous agents.

## Why This Matters for VeriLinkOS

The Cordis framework formalized two orthogonal dimensions of composability:

- **Temporal composability**: The ability to completely revert a component's side effects upon removal.
- **Spatial composability**: The ability to declare and manage inter-component dependencies reactively.

VeriLinkOS extends this formal foundation with cryptographic identity, cryptographic proof, cross-organizational trust, and agent-to-agent commerce, applying it specifically to the domain of autonomous AI agents.
